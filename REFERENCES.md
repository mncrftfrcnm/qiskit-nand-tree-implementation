# References

This file keeps the broader reading list separate from the main README. The first group is the most directly relevant to the NAND-tree implementation; the later groups provide background on formula evaluation, continuous-time oracles, Hamiltonian simulation, and classical tree evaluation.

## NAND trees and quantum formula evaluation

1. Edward Farhi, Jeffrey Goldstone, and Sam Gutmann. *A Quantum Algorithm for the Hamiltonian NAND Tree*. 2008. [Theory of Computing](https://theoryofcomputing.org/articles/v004a008/)

2. Andrew M. Childs, Richard Cleve, Stephen P. Jordan, and David Yonge-Mallo. *Discrete-Query Quantum Algorithm for NAND Trees*. 2009. [Theory of Computing](https://theoryofcomputing.org/articles/v005a005/)

3. Andris Ambainis, Andrew M. Childs, Ben W. Reichardt, Robert Špalek, and Shengyu Zhang. *Any AND-OR Formula of Size N Can Be Evaluated in Time N^(1/2+o(1)) on a Quantum Computer*. 2007. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0703015)

4. Ben W. Reichardt and Robert Špalek. *Span-Program-Based Quantum Algorithm for Evaluating Formulas*. 2007. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.2630)

5. Ben W. Reichardt. *Span-Program-Based Quantum Algorithm for Evaluating Unbalanced Formulas*. 2009. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.1622)

6. Ben W. Reichardt. *Span Programs and Quantum Query Complexity: The General Adversary Bound Is Nearly Tight for Every Boolean Function*. 2009. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.2759)

## Earlier quantum-walk and oracle background

7. Edward Farhi and Sam Gutmann. *Quantum Computation and Decision Trees*. 1998. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9706062)

8. Edward Farhi and Sam Gutmann. *An Analog Analogue of a Digital Quantum Computation*. 1998. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9612026)

9. Carlos Mochon. *Hamiltonian Oracles*. 2007. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0602032)

10. Dominic W. Berry, Graeme Ahokas, Richard Cleve, and Barry C. Sanders. *Efficient Quantum Algorithms for Simulating Sparse Hamiltonians*. 2007. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0508139)

## Query lower bounds and related tree problems

11. Howard Barnum and Michael Saks. *A Lower Bound on the Quantum Query Complexity of Read-Once Functions*. 2004. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0201007)

12. Peter Høyer, Troy Lee, and Robert Špalek. *Tight Adversary Bounds for Composite Functions*. 2005. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0509067)

13. Richard Cleve, Dmytro Gavinsky, and David L. Yonge-Mallo. *Quantum Algorithms for Evaluating MIN-MAX Trees*. 2007. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.5794)

## Classical tree-evaluation background

15. Michael Saks and Avi Wigderson. *Probabilistic Boolean Decision Trees and the Complexity of Evaluating Game Trees*. 1986. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/SFCS.1986.44)
